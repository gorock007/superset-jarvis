⏺ I've read the spec and I'm stopping before writing code, because two of its
  requirements cannot both be met and the choice affects the file format that
  Finance will depend on.

  1. "The export must stream" and "the first row is a totals row". A totals
     row at the top means reading every row before sending the first byte.
     Options: put the totals row LAST; or run a separate SUM() query first
     (cheap, one extra query) and still stream the rows.

  2. "Opens in Excel with umlauts intact" and "UTF-8 without BOM". Excel on
     Windows needs the BOM to detect UTF-8. Options: write the BOM (pandas
     copes with encoding="utf-8-sig"); or no BOM and Finance imports through
     Data > From Text.

  My recommendation is SUM() first plus a BOM. But this is your call, not
  mine, since both change what downstream users see.

  How do you want to resolve 1 and 2?
